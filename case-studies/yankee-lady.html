One system, four connected stages

The challenge: Yankee Lady serves two different audiences: people planning social events and companies booking corporate cruises. Marketing had to reach both, and bring past guests back season after season.

The solution: I organized the work into four connected stages, built each channel around how people actually plan and book a cruise, and tracked every one against bookings.
