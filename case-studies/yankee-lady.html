01 · The challenge

Filling cruises for both private and corporate events

Yankee Lady serves two different audiences: people planning social events and companies booking corporate cruises. Marketing had to reach both, and bring past guests back season after season. [Add the starting point: rankings, leads or bookings before you joined.]

02 · What I did

Search, paid and email, tuned for bookings

I built each channel around how people actually plan and book a cruise, then tracked every one against bookings.

  1. Built an SEO strategy from the ground up

    Resolved indexing issues, improved site performance, ran competitive and keyword analysis, optimized on-page elements and strengthened the backlink profile. 105 keywords reached Google's top 10.

    • SEO
    • Technical SEO

  2. Managed a $20K/month Google Ads program

    Designed the top 5 converting landing pages, segmented keyword themes, refined negative keywords and A/B tested ad copy. Outbound lead generation rose 20%.

    • PPC
    • Landing pages

  3. Segmented 10,000+ email subscribers

    Split the list into B2C and B2B groups, built automated workflows, and wrote personalized monthly newsletters and promotions. Repeat bookings for upcoming seasons rose 5%.

    • Email
03 · The outcome

More leads, more repeat guests, clearer decisions

Search visibility, paid leads and repeat bookings all grew together. I tracked campaign KPIs closely to reallocate budget, turned the data into easy-to-read dashboards, and kept marketing, sales and management working from the same numbers.
