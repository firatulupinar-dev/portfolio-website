// THE GROWTH ROADMAP

One system, four connected stages

The challenge: Proco needed to reach a specialist B2B audience of plastics and packaging manufacturers, grow the sales pipeline across digital and traditional channels, and give C-level executives clear data on where to invest.

The solution: I organized the work into four connected stages, from getting found by the right buyers to measuring what worked, so every channel had a clear job in the funnel.

  1. 01Attract
  2. 02Convert
  3. 03Retain
  4. 04Measure
Data-driven decisions across the customer journey

Get found by plastics and packaging buyers

What I did

  • Technical SEO & Research: Fixed the site’s technical SEO and researched the keywords and competitors buyers actually search.
  • On-page & Authority: Optimized every key page and earned high-quality backlinks from the industry.
  • Industry Content & Events: Wrote articles for industry magazines and supported trade shows and print to reach buyers offline.
Results
+200%
organic traffic

Tools

  • Semrush
  • Screaming Frog
  • Google Search Console

Turn interest into quotes and orders

What I did

  • Targeted Paid Campaigns: Ran Google Ads, LinkedIn and industry-network campaigns, segmented by industry and company size.
  • Testing & Remarketing: A/B-tested creatives and landing pages to find the messages that moved buyers, and used remarketing to re-engage prospects who had already visited.
  • Website Redesign: Redesigned the website around how buyers research and request quotes.
  • Lead Capture: Added lead forms and clear calls to action for automation enquiries and part orders, and connected the new traffic to the sales team, contributing to $1M+ in automation sales.
Results
+10%
marketing-qualified leads
+30%
part sales

Tools

  • Google Ads
  • LinkedIn Ads

Keep customers coming back

What I did

  • Segmentation: Segmented customer accounts by industry and company size.
  • Email Automation: Built automated email flows and industry-specific newsletters.
  • CRM Integration: Integrated the CRM with Mailchimp so sales and marketing shared one view of each account.
Results
+5%
customer retention

Tools

  • Mailchimp

Show leadership what marketing delivered

What I did

  • KPIs for Every Campaign: Defined and tracked the KPIs for every campaign and reported them to the C-suite.
  • Budget Follows Results: Moved budget to what was working, so leadership could see what marketing delivered at every stage.
