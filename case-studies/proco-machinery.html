01 · The challenge

Reaching plastics and packaging manufacturers across North America

Proco sells robotic automation to a specialist B2B audience. The job was to grow brand awareness and the sales pipeline across digital and traditional channels, and to give C-level executives clear data to decide where to invest. [Add the starting point: traffic, leads or sales before you joined.]

02 · What I did

One omnichannel B2B program, built on a stronger website

I connected search, paid media, email, content and events so every channel fed the same pipeline.

  1. Redesigned the website and fixed its SEO foundations

    Resolved technical issues, researched keywords and competitors, optimized on-page elements, built high-quality backlinks and added lead forms. Organic traffic grew 200%+.

    • Technical SEO
    • Web design

  2. Ran targeted paid campaigns

    Directed Google Ads, LinkedIn and industry-network campaigns with audience segmentation, A/B-tested landing pages and creatives, and remarketing to re-engage prospects. Marketing-qualified leads rose 10%.

    • PPC
    • Remarketing

  3. Built industry-specific email and content

    Segmented accounts by industry and size, built automated flows and newsletters, integrated the CRM with Mailchimp, and wrote articles for industry magazines. Customer retention improved 5%.

    • Email
    • Content
03 · The outcome

A bigger pipeline and clearer decisions

Organic traffic grew more than 200%, part sales rose 30% and marketing-qualified leads increased 10%, all contributing to a $1M+ boost in automation sales. I defined and tracked the KPIs for every campaign, so budget moved to what was working and executives had the insights to act on.
