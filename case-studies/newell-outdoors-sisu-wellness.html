// THE GROWTH ROADMAP

One system, four connected stages

The challenge: Newell sells in a crowded outdoor category, where shoppers compare products and prices across marketplaces before they buy. The store needed more of the right shoppers finding it, more of them checking out, and more of them coming back, with digital working alongside print and trade shows.

The solution: I ran the store as one funnel: search and content to bring shoppers in, paid campaigns built to sell rather than just drive clicks, email to win the second order, and one scorecard to decide where the next dollar went.

Data-driven decisions across the customer journey

Get found by shoppers already searching for the gear

What I did

  • Keyword Research: Ran keyword research and a competitive gap analysis to build an SEO-driven editorial calendar.
  • SEO-Driven Redesign: Rebuilt the store on a clean site structure, with collections mapped to how shoppers search and fast pages that are easy to crawl and navigate.
  • Technical & On-page SEO: Fixed indexing and page-speed issues, then optimized existing pages’ titles, meta descriptions, headings and internal links.
  • Content: Crafted content for product pages, landing pages, blog posts, how-to pages and infographics around the terms shoppers use when comparing gear.
  • Link Building: Earned backlinks that gave category and product pages the authority to compete with bigger retailers.
  • Local SEO: Optimized the Google Business Profile and local listings.
  • Google Shopping: Optimized Google Merchant Center and maintained the SKU feed for Google Shopping.
  • Paid Search & Social: Ran targeted Google and Meta ads on a $15K+ monthly budget.

Turn ad spend into profitable orders

What I did

  • Audience Segmentation: Split Google Ads and Meta campaigns by audience and buying intent, and gave each segment its own keywords, creative and landing page.
  • Conversion Path: Built a conversion path from top of funnel to checkout, with clear CTAs and internal linking at every step.
  • Remarketing: Ran remarketing campaigns to bring back visitors and cart abandoners who didn’t buy on the first visit.
  • A/B Testing: Tested landing pages, ad creatives and copy on a steady cadence, cutting losers early and moving budget to winners.
  • Profit over Clicks: Judged every campaign on return on ad spend and cost per acquisition, not clicks or impressions.
  • List Growth: Used paid campaigns to grow the email list alongside sales, so we could reach new visitors again without paying for the click twice.

Turn first orders into repeat customers

What I did

  • List Building: Grew the subscriber list with downloadable how-to guides and infographics.
  • Segmentation: Segmented contacts by location and interest to send personalized emails.
  • Automated Flows: Built welcome, abandoned-cart and post-purchase flows in Klaviyo that kept selling between campaigns.
  • Review Nurture: Built a review nurture email cycle that turned buyers into reviewers.
  • Campaigns & Testing: Sent 40+ campaigns, A/B testing templates, content and calls to action, and folded each winner into the next send.
Results
+25%

+10%

Tools

  • Klaviyo
  • Shopify

Tie every channel to one north star metric: online sales

What I did

  • SMART Goals: Set SMART goals for each channel and optimized campaigns based on performance against them.
  • Monthly Reporting: Delivered monthly traffic, keyword, email and sales reports to stakeholders in Looker, showing what moved sales and what didn’t.
  • One Scorecard: Measured SEO, paid and email against the same KPIs: revenue, return on ad spend, cost per acquisition, conversion rate and repeat purchases.
  • Insights to Action: Used the numbers to shift budget between channels, rework underperforming pages and plan the next round of tests.
