01 · The challenge

Moving more shoppers from first click to purchase

Two D2C ecommerce brands in different categories shared one goal: guide more prospects through the marketing funnel, using digital channels alongside print and trade shows. [Add the starting point: sales, ROAS or traffic before you joined.]

02 · What I did

Paid, organic and email working as one funnel

Each channel had a clear job in the funnel, and every campaign was measured against the same KPIs.

  1. Rebuilt paid campaigns around the audience

    Managed Google Ads and Meta campaigns, segmenting audiences and matching content, landing pages and keywords to each one, with constant A/B testing. The result: 3× ROAS, a 20% lower CPA and more email subscribers.

    • PPC
    • A/B testing

  2. Modernized the site and its SEO

    Modernized the web design, fixed indexing and performance issues, optimized on-page elements and strengthened backlinks. I also led content for product pages, landing pages, blogs and infographics. Organic traffic grew 40%, bounce rate fell 20% and average engagement reached 2 minutes.

    • SEO
    • Content

  3. Built segmented email flows

    Split subscribers into smaller target groups, built automated flows and created 40+ campaigns, A/B testing templates, content and calls to action. Open rates rose 25% and customer retention 10%.

    • Email automation
03 · The outcome

30% more online sales, and the data to keep improving

Online sales grew 30% year over year as paid, organic and email each pulled their weight. Regular analysis of customer and campaign KPIs in Looker gave stakeholders clear trends and insights to make data-driven decisions.
