// HOW I GREW IT

Four stages, one connected system

Accuenergy sells technical products to engineers, facility managers and utilities, with a steady stream of new launches. I ran marketing across the whole buyer journey: attract the right buyers, convert them into leads, keep them engaged, and measure what worked.

Skills usedSEO · Content & PR · Google Ads · Social media · Landing pages & CRO · Email & CRM · Analytics & reporting

01 Attract

Bring the right buyers to every launch

Get technical products found by the engineers and buyers already searching for them.

+25%
website traffic, year over year
+40%
LinkedIn followers, year over year
  • SEO strategy

    Keyword research, content gap analysis and keyword-to-page mapping for metering and monitoring searches

  • On-page & technical SEO

    Optimized title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal links and image alt text, and added structured data (schema markup) so search engines and AI answers could read each product page correctly

  • Content & PR

    Product pages, blog articles, video scripts and press releases for 15+ launches

  • Paid search

    Google Ads on every launch, reaching active buyers from day one while SEO built long-term traffic

  • Social media

    Annual content calendar across launches, trade shows, industry events and company news

02 Convert

Give every visitor a clear, consistent path from search to quote request.

340
leads a month, on average
  • Campaign optimization

    Segmented campaigns by product and intent; tuned bids, budgets, negative keywords and ad schedules

  • Landing pages

    Matched each ad's promise from search to form, and A/B-tested value propositions, CTAs and form placement

  • Conversion paths

    Internal links and focused calls to action that moved readers from educational content to product pages

  • Lead tracking

    UTMs on every campaign, so each lead traced back to the channel and campaign that produced it
